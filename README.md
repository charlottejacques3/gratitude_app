# Gratitude Buddy


## About

A regular gratitude practice is one of the most effective techniques for improving happiness and life satisfaction. This is why I developed Gratitude Buddy, a mobile app designed to help users find gratitude in their day-to-day lives. The app is designed for Android, although it is built using Flutter to allow for future cross-platform compatibility. Firebase was used as a backend. To promote habit formation, the app reminds the user to log their gratitude daily, either by random or scheduled notifications. Their logs can take the form of text or images.
However, unlike many gratitude apps currently available, this program also recognizes that it is not always easy to think of things to be grateful for, especially when we are going through a hard time. To help combat this, the app implements multiple tools to help users get inspired or reframe their negative thoughts, with an emphasis on providing multiple levels of support depending on the user’s needs. For example, a lower level of support would consist of simply providing ideas and inspiration. A higher level of support would be a full guided process, allowing the user to log their negative emotions, identify cognitive distortions, and reframe their thoughts in a more positive manner. The positive reframing element makes use of an AI model to generate fill-in-the-blank reframing prompts based on the user’s specific situation.
Finally, an increased depth in one’s gratitude practice can have an even further positive impact on life satisfaction. This project takes advantage of this by implementing three guided reflection strategies to allow the user to think more deeply about what they are grateful for and how it affects their life.
